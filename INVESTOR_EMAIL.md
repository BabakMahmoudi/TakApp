WHAT TAK IS

TAK began with a simple observation in a small café: the wholesale price of coffee is far below what any single café, or any single person, pays for it. TAK turns that idea into a working system. It is a SEP-41 token on Stellar that acts as a digital coupon and membership share in a community-owned coffee supply chain. Members pool their purchasing power to buy (and eventually import) coffee at wholesale, and the savings flow back to members as a real, per-cup discount — up to roughly 30% off every cup, without squeezing the café's margin.

WHY IT CAN GROW

The opportunity is structural, not speculative. Independent cafés today buy roasted coffee at roughly 30–50% above the bulk price a pooled buyer can reach. TAK captures that spread: every TAK in circulation is fully backed by 20 g of roasted coffee held in the community store, giving the token a hard floor and an inflation hedge. As members join, larger pooled purchases unlock better wholesale terms and bigger discounts — a virtuous cycle that scales from a single café to a region, then to a national network of participating shops. A small, governance-adjustable transaction fee funds the organization at each stage.

TRY IT YOURSELF

The wallet is fully non-custodial — no one but you holds your keys or your funds.
